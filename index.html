01 — Our Story

Built from a real problem.

I'm Aaravan Balachander, a Class 10 student at Vellore International School, Chennai. After repeatedly discovering competitions, conferences and experiences through scattered sources and personal connections, I realised that valuable opportunities are often missed simply because people hear about them too late.

SkillBridge was created to make opportunity discovery more accessible and connected — bringing students looking to grow, schools creating experiences and organisers offering them into one ecosystem.

More opportunities should never depend on who you happen to know.

Aaravan Balachander

Founder & Student Builder

Class 10 · Vellore International School · Chennai
